Programme Areas

Forest Restoration

Our flagship programme rehabilitates degraded tropical forests through native species reforestation, invasive alien species removal, and community-based forest management. We work in 6 forest ecosystems across Kenya, using GPS monitoring and citizen science to ensure seedling survival rates above 80%.

Water Conservation

Clean water is a fundamental right. We protect watershed integrity through riparian buffer restoration, riverbank rehabilitation, and sustainable water harvesting. Our WASH component delivers clean water access to over 85,000 people who previously walked 5+ km daily to water sources.

Clean Energy Access

Replacing charcoal and kerosene with solar microgrids and biogas digesters transforms household economics, reduces deforestation pressure, and improves indoor air quality. We've installed 340 solar home systems and 12 community microgrids, while training 280 local solar technicians for a sustainable maintenance ecosystem.

Eco-Education

Environmental literacy is the foundation of all future action. We integrate ecology into school curricula, run youth Green Champions leadership camps, and train teachers across 120 partner schools. Our "Eco-Schools" certification programme has transformed 35 schools into living sustainability laboratories.

Biodiversity Protection

Kenya's wildlife corridors are fragmented and under pressure. We design and implement wildlife passage restoration, pollinator habitat corridors, and community wildlife conservancies that turn former poachers into conservation champions. Our ranger programme has reduced wildlife crime by 68% in partner areas.

Green Communities

Sustainability must be lived at home. Our urban and peri-urban greening programme creates composting networks, urban food forests, climate-smart agriculture training, and waste reduction systems that turn communities into circular economy hubs. 28 community gardens now supply 40% of local food needs.
