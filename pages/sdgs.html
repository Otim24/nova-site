12 Goals, One Mission

Green economies and community conservation provide supplemental income to 8,200 families.

Food security improved
65% of households
Climate-smart agriculture and community gardens serve 28 communities with diversified food production.
3
Good Health
Health outcomes improved
88% reduction in waterborne illness
Clean water access and replacing biomass fuels dramatically reduces respiratory and waterborne diseases.
4
Quality Education
Schools engaged
120 of 150 target schools
Eco-Education programme reaches 45,000+ students with environmental literacy curriculum.

Clean Water
People with clean water
91% of households in target zones
85,000+ people now access clean water within 500m of their homes through our WASH programme.

Clean Energy
Households with clean energy
78% — 12,000+ households

10
Reduced Inequalities
Marginalized communities prioritized
85% of programmes in high-need areas
Deliberate targeting of pastoralist, indigenous, and other marginalized communities in all programmes.
11
Sustainable Cities
Urban communities served
14 urban zones active
Urban greening, composting networks, and green corridors in 14 Nairobi and Mombasa neighbourhoods.
13
Climate Action
Communities climate-resilient
70% — 30 of 42 communities
Climate adaptation plans co-designed with 30 communities. 540 tonnes CO₂ sequestered annually.

14 freshwater systems
Riverbank restoration protects 14 freshwater ecosystems including Lake Victoria tributaries.

6 major forest ecosystems
Our flagship programme — 500,000 trees planted, 6 ecosystems under active restoration, 240 species protected.
