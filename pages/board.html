At The Helm

Executive Leadership

The passionate leaders who translate our mission into action every single day.

Executive Director

Dr. Amina Osei

Ph.D. Environmental Science, University of Nairobi

Dr. Amina Osei has dedicated 18 years to environmental conservation across East and West Africa. Before founding Nova Green Foundation, she led ecosystem restoration programmes for WWF East Africa and advised the Kenyan government on forestry policy.

Under her leadership, Nova Green has grown from a 5-person volunteer team to a 85-staff organization with a $4.2M annual portfolio. She holds a Ph.D. in Environmental Science and is a member of the IUCN Commission on Ecosystem Management.
