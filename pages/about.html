OUR MISSION

To drive sustainable, market-led green economic transformation across Africa.

Nova Green Economy Foundation empowers communities, enterprises, and local institutions to adopt regenerative economic systems. We bridge sustainable capital, climate technologies, and grassroots capacity to scale food security, circular value chains, renewable energy commerce, and climate resilience.

OUR VISION

A thriving, self-sustaining green economy across Africa by 2030.

We envision an equitable continental economy where green enterprise is the cornerstone of shared prosperity. By aligning business productivity with environmental stewardship, we foster resilient communities where youth, women, and rural producers thrive within balanced, restored ecosystems.

Our Story

Born from a Burning Forest

In 2019, a group of ecologists, community organizers, and youth activists witnessed the devastation of the Mau Forest complex — East Africa's largest montane forest, losing 25,000 hectares every year to encroachment and charcoal burning.

Rather than despair, they organized. Nova Green Foundation was registered that same year with a simple mandate: act with urgency, act with evidence, act with community.

Seven years on, we operate across 8 counties in Kenya, with affiliate programmes in Uganda, Tanzania, and Rwanda. Our team of 85 staff and 2,000+ volunteers represents some of the region's brightest environmental minds — united by the conviction that a green future is not only possible, but necessary.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

01

Regenerative Enterprise

Fostering market-viable business models that restore ecosystems rather than depleting them, aligning profitability with natural renewal.

02

Community Co-Ownership

Sustainable transformation succeeds when local producers lead. We co-create interventions that anchor economic sovereignty in communities.

03

Evidence & Rigorous Metrics

Empirical data, verified carbon baselines, and transparent socioeconomic tracking govern our resource allocation and impact reporting.

04

Equitable Green Growth

Prioritizing smallholder farmers, youth innovators, and women entrepreneurs who bear disproportionate climate burdens.

05

Circular Systems Thinking

Eliminating waste through closed-loop agricultural and industrial value chains, transforming byproducts into economic value.

06

Pragmatic Capital Mobilization

Connecting grassroots green initiatives to blended finance, climate funds, and impact investors with institutional governance.

Our Journey

Milestones That Shaped Us

From a small volunteer group in the Aberdares to a pan-African force for ecological renewal — here are the pivotal moments that defined Nova Green Foundation.

2019 • Genesis

Foundation Established

Registered as a non-profit environmental NGO in Nairobi, Kenya. Our very first community tree-planting mobilisation rallied 150 volunteers and planted 3,500 indigenous seedlings across degraded ridges of the Aberdare mountain range.

150 Volunteers • Aberdare Range Reforestation
2020 • Global Multilateral Entry

First UN Partnership

Selected as accredited implementing partner for the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) Global Environment Facility (GEF) Small Grants Programme. Launched our flagship Water Conservation Initiative protecting 8 highland river basin catchments.

UNDP / GEF • 8 River Basins Protected
2021 • Scale & Regional Expansion

100,000 Trees Milestone

Celebrated the planting and survival verification of our 100,000th indigenous tree. Extended regional cross-border operations into Uganda and Tanzania, and launched the Youth Eco-Education pilot curriculum across 20 rural primary schools.

100k Geotagged Trees • 20 Eco-Schools
2023 • Clean Energy Infrastructure

Clean Energy Access Launch

Commissioned our first solar-powered microgrids and community water pumping systems in arid Turkana County. The programme now delivers clean electricity and potable water to over 12,000 pastoralist households, displacing diesel generation.

12,000 Households • Solar Microgrids & Pumps
2025 • Policy & Continental Recognition

UNEP Strategic Partnership

Signed a landmark multi-year MOU with the UNEP East Africa Regional Hub to scale ecosystem monitoring, digital MRV standards, and community conservation corridors. Recognized as "Best Practice Environmental NGO" by Kenya's Ministry of Environment.

UNEP Strategic MOU • National Best Practice Award
2026 • Vision 2030 Horizon

500,000 Trees & Beyond

Crossed the landmark of 500,000 verified surviving trees, directly benefiting 42 community cooperatives across East Africa. Unveiled our ambitious Vision 2030 Roadmap: 2 million trees planted, 50,000 green jobs catalyzed, and net-zero regional operations.

500k Trees Milestone • 42 Cooperatives • Vision 2030
