Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

We welcome volunteers at all levels! Field volunteers join planting events and community programmes (requires physical fitness and safety briefing). Office volunteers support communications, research, and administration. Simply fill in the contact form above selecting "Volunteering" and we'll be in touch within 3 business days.
Donations can be made via M-Pesa (Paybill: 000000), bank transfer, or online through our payment portal (link on contact form). 82 cents of every dollar donated goes directly to programmes, with 18% for operations and administration. We publish full financial audits annually — available on request.
We offer flexible partnership models including: programme sponsorship, employee volunteering days, carbon offset partnerships, co-branded conservation campaigns, and strategic alliances for shared advocacy. All partnerships include impact reporting aligned with your ESG reporting requirements.
Yes. Nova Green Foundation is fully registered under Kenya's NGO Coordination Act (Reg. No. NGO/12345/2019), audited annually by a certified public accounting firm, and compliant with the Public Benefit Organizations Act. We are also registered with OECD DAC in the process of IATI reporting.
Yes. We have affiliate programmes in Uganda, Tanzania, and Rwanda, and are pursuing registration in Ethiopia and DRC. We also partner with organizations across 28 countries through our SDG-aligned partnerships network. Our research and policy advocacy work has a global reach through publications and UN engagement.
We aim to respond to all enquiries within 3 business days. For urgent media enquiries, please email media@novagreen.org directly and mark your subject line URGENT. For programme partnership enquiries, initial scoping calls are typically scheduled within 1 week.
