Partnership Case Studies

Exemplary multilateral and corporate collaborations delivering audited, generational outcomes for African landscapes.

Multilateral Strategic

UNEP East Africa Canopy & Digital MRV Facility

In 2025, Nova Green entered a formal memorandum with UNEP's regional hub to pilot high-resolution remote sensing verification for indigenous tree species survival across high-altitude cloud forests.

1.2M Ha Audited Canopy
94.2% Tree Survival Rate

Key Mandate Outcomes:

  • Automated drone lidar scanning integrated with global UNEP biodiversity database.
  • Direct open-source GIS repository accessible to university researchers.
  • Eliminated self-reporting bias through third-party sovereign verification audits.
Global Facility Grant

UNDP GEF Small Grants: Highland Water Catchments

Initiated in 2020, this partnership mobilized 18 community water user associations (WRUAs) across Aberdare rivers to rehabilitate deforested riparian margins through agroforestry tree buffers.

18 WRUAs Basin Coalitions
34% Less Silt Runoff

Key Mandate Outcomes:

  • Restored 340 kilometers of critical riverbank vegetation buffer.
  • Constructed 24 gravity-fed solar filtration taps eliminating water-borne illnesses.
  • Established self-sustaining community nursery enterprises generating local revenue.
Corporate ESG Innovation

Safaricom Foundation: IoT Forest Alert Grid

A pioneering fusion of telecommunications technology and conservation ecology. Low-power IoT nodes installed across mountain ridges continuously sniff for trace volatile organic smoke compounds.

< 8 Mins Alert Dispatch
Zero Catastrophic Fires

Key Mandate Outcomes:

  • 120 connected environmental telemetry nodes active across vulnerable forest borders.
  • M-Pesa micropayments distributed directly to women tree stewards upon photo survival audits.
  • Protected 45,000 hectares of young regenerating saplings from illegal encroachment.
