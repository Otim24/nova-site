Who We Are

A Force for
Environmental Good

Nova Green Foundation was born from a simple conviction: every action for the environment counts. We bridge science, community, and policy to create lasting ecological change across East Africa and beyond.

From restoring degraded forests to enabling clean energy access in rural areas, our multidisciplinary approach ensures that sustainability becomes a living reality — not just an aspiration.

Systems Thinking

We address root causes, not symptoms — building resilient ecosystems at every level.

Community-Led

Local wisdom and global expertise combined for solutions that actually last.

Data-Driven Impact

Every programme is measured, reported and refined for maximum ecological return.

What We Do

Our Impact Programmes

Six transformative pillars of action spanning conservation, energy, water, and education.

Forest Restoration
Rehabilitating degraded ecosystems through native species planting, invasive species removal, and long-term forest stewardship programmes.
Water Conservation
Protecting watershed integrity, restoring riverbanks, and delivering clean water solutions to underserved rural communities.
Clean Energy Access
Installing solar microgrids and biogas systems, replacing charcoal with sustainable cooking solutions for 10,000+ households.
Eco-Education
School curriculum integration, teacher training, and youth environmental leadership camps across 120 partner schools.
Biodiversity Protection
Wildlife corridor creation, pollinator habitat restoration, and community-based conservation ranger programmes.
Green Communities
Urban greening, waste reduction, composting networks, and climate-resilient agriculture for food-secure communities.
